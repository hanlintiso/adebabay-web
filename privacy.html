Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Adebabay ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Adebabay mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name and email address when you register
  • Listing data: photos, descriptions, prices, and contact details you post
  • Contact information: phone number if provided in listings or support requests
  • Messages: in-app communications between users
  • Usage data: pages viewed and interactions within the app (collected anonymously)

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To publish and display your listings
  • To facilitate communication between buyers and sellers
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the app and user experience

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Adebabay:

  • Supabase — secure cloud database and authentication
  • Expo — app delivery and updates
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
